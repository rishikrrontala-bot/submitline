# LovHack Season 3 win contract

## Clock

- Event deadline: **October 4, 2026, 11:45 p.m. EDT**.
- Protected submission cutoff: **October 4, 2026, 9:45 p.m. EDT**. The Devpost entry, public repo, live demo, and public 2–3 minute video must be verified before then.
- Build period: September 26–October 4, 2026. This repository starts during that window; any imported work must be disclosed.

## Rubric-to-proof map

| Criterion | Weight | Proof judges should see |
|---|---:|---|
| Execution | 35% | A real end-to-end check, safe link fetching, independent reruns, reliable export, tests. |
| Problem and impact | 25% | A concrete missed-submission failure and a blocked-to-fixed walkthrough. |
| Innovation | 20% | A judge-view evidence board that distinguishes observed facts from model interpretation and human review. |
| Presentation and UX | 20% | One clear workspace, strong visual hierarchy, accessible 2–3 minute demo. |

## Winner research

LovHack Season 2's verified winners include [Drygate, first place](https://devpost.com/software/nadi-ai-health-context-engine), [OmniBraille, second](https://devpost.com/software/omnibraille), [Lumina, third](https://devpost.com/software/lumina-brl9dw), and Builder Award projects [FlowForge](https://devpost.com/software/flowforge-r6j5ds) and [PlayWeaver](https://devpost.com/software/playweaver). Their pages show concrete pain and visible end-to-end transformations. Drygate's quality-gate framing is especially close, so Submitline's differentiator must be its source-linked judge view and honest uncertainty, not a generic checklist.

## Submission contract

The [official event page](https://lovhack-season-3.devpost.com/) and [rules](https://lovhack-season-3.devpost.com/rules) require a Devpost description, technologies used, a 2–3 minute demo accessible to judges, and disclosure of reused work. A working demo is requested whenever possible. A GitHub repository is an example of a shareable prototype, **not a standalone required artifact**. Submitline checks one when provided without marking its absence as a LovHack violation.

## Stop-ship conditions

Broken or permission-gated demo/video; missing required text; fabricated video evidence; undisclosed reuse; a product demo that omits the actual blocked-to-fixed check.
